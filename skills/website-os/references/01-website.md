# Website

Open the supplied references before styling. Identify what each contributes: page rhythm, typography, navigation density, asset treatment or one signature interaction. Combine the useful parts and record the choices in the System view. Respect a user's request to follow one exact reference; do not require an arbitrary number of URLs.

The Website OS workspace opens on the actual functioning homepage, with small supporting tabs. Keep the approved homepage's design and scripts when wrapping an existing site. Lead with the site's actual job. A tool opens on its working surface. A marketing page opens on the product and its concrete outcome. Use a clear focal point, deliberate type scale and sections that each earn their space. Avoid turning every section into a matching three-card grid.

Make a coherent first version, then inspect its real layout. Adapt at 1440, 1280, 768 and 375 pixels without horizontal page overflow. At narrow sizes, stack the inspector beneath the canvas or use a readable drawer. Keep controls reachable without obscuring the page.

Use motion to explain change or support the chosen art direction. Respect reduced motion. Scroll-driven scenes from the legacy shell are available, but they are not mandatory for dashboards or every new brand. Prevent entrance animations from hiding content if JavaScript fails.

Use semantic headings, visible focus states and labelled controls. Four-system tabs must support keyboard navigation, keep one active panel, and restore sensible focus. A copied scaffold is unfinished until every placeholder, missing asset and unreachable button has been resolved.

Stable content IDs belong on meaningful pieces of copy, not on every decorative span. Preserve decorative headline markup by putting separate fields inside its leaf spans. Keep prices, links, legal text and SEO identifiable. The starter shows this binding pattern.
